# Chronoframe — App Store preview

## Direction

A calm, confident demonstration of control over a photo library. Real application footage,
large benefit-led captions, an original instrumental score, and restrained dissolves.
No voiceover. The narrative must remain understandable with sound muted.

The first deliverable is a 29.8-second Mac App Store preview at 1920×1080, 30 fps.
Apple accepts 15–30 seconds and 1920×1080 for Mac previews; the requested longer 4K
website/social film is a separate follow-up, not an App Store upload format.

## Story

| Time | Customer benefit | Evidence shown |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Give years of memories a clear home | A real 4,000-file preview and date timeline |
| 5–10 s | Organize without changing source originals | Completed transfer, verified-copy workflow, run summary |
| 10–16.8 s | Decide which version to keep | Actual side-by-side duplicate comparison and Keep selection |
| 16.8–20 s | Clear extras with control | One reviewed file and the Trash confirmation |
| 20–24.2 s | Change your mind | History receipt and restore confirmation |
| 24.2–27.3 s | Get a file back | Successful restoration from Trash |
| 27.3–29.8 s | Your library, your control | Return to the visual comparison with the closing promise |

## Visual treatment

Keep the app as the visual evidence throughout. A restrained dark caption rail covers the
left navigation/thumbnail column; it is clearly editorial, never a simulated app control.
The rail also excludes unrelated sample-photo thumbnails from the finished film. Retain
the public-domain sandboarding photograph in the comparison. White type, warm amber
waypoint accents, no device mockups, stock lifestyle footage, pricing, or external CTA.
Use straightforward dissolves and fixed close-ups of the real footage to make key text
readable. Do not manufacture cursor clicks or speed claims.

## Delivery checks

- H.264 High, Level 4.0; progressive yuv420p; BT.709; 30 fps; 1920×1080.
- Under 30 seconds and 500 MB; target 10–12 Mbps video.
- AAC stereo, 48 kHz, 256 kbps; original music; no unlicensed commercial recording.
- Readable captions, no personal paths, no unrelated apps or desktop.
- Verify representative frames, transitions, beginning/end, frame count and audio loudness.
- Deliver poster, caption transcript, music master, silent video and reproducible edit source.
