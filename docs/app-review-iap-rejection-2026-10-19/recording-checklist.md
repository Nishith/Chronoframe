# TestFlight 508 purchase recording

recorded. Preserve that account and its purchase. Use a second fresh sandbox tester
for a recorded new purchase; do not clear purchase history, allowance, preferences
or bookmarks.

## Prepare before filming

1. Finish creating the second tester in App Store Connect using an unused email
   or alias and a password entered directly by the owner. Do not send credentials
   in chat or include them in the video.
2. Switch only the sandbox purchasing identity using the Mac's actual supported
   sign-in UI. Keep the main iCloud identity unchanged. Verify TestFlight 2.0 (508)
   resolves to Free trial and loads the real Chronoframe Unlock product. Merely
   creating the tester does not switch the Mac's sandbox account.
3. Prepare one disposable source fixture and a new empty destination. The prior
   Review 508 Destination already contains its verified copy, so repeating a preview
   there may correctly find nothing to copy. Keep the earlier receipt and files.
4. Have the phone camera ready. In this session the native purchase footer was
   absent during Mac screen capture and returned after capture stopped; the exact
   cause is unproven. Keep Mac screen recording off for this attempt. External
   camera footage is alternative physical-Mac evidence; Apple's acceptance of it
   in place of its requested screen recording remains to be confirmed.

## Capture one continuous demonstration

1. Start phone video before launching Chronoframe from the desktop. Frame the Mac
   display clearly and avoid unrelated windows or personal files.
2. Show About: version 2.0, build 508.
3. Select the disposable source and empty destination, preview, confirm a verified
   transfer, then show the completed History receipt and Match verification.
4. Show the locked Reorganize purchase entrance without committing any moves;
   dismiss it and restore any changed layout preference. Other paid entrances use
   the same non-consumable, not another SKU. Any unrecorded entrance remains an
   explicit gap; do not claim the clip demonstrates a flow it does not show.
5. Open Settings → License → Unlock Chronoframe…. Show the localized product price,
   Restore Purchases, Apple's no-charge testing notice and the final Purchase
   button. Confirm the sandbox purchase locally. Keep credentials out of frame.
6. Capture Apple's success message, dismiss it, then show License → Unlocked.
   Quit/relaunch and show Unlocked again. Stop the phone video and transfer the file
   to the Mac; retain its original and record the exact local path.

If Apple returns an incomplete or looping sign-in dialog, stop the test and retain
the failure details rather than claiming success. If a real purchase succeeds,
that tester now owns the unlock; an already-owned response does not demonstrate a
second fresh purchase.
