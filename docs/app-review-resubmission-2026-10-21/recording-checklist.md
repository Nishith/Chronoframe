3. Use disposable fixtures under `/private/tmp/Chronoframe-Review-20261021/`; the baseline is retained locally. Preserve prior 508 evidence. Confirm the core demo destination is empty before filming, or prepare a new empty one.
4. Set up a screen recording of the display so Apple's separate purchase sheet can be included. Apple's actual rejection requests a screen recording from a physical device. External camera footage remains a fallback with acceptance unconfirmed. General Apple guidance about hardware demo videos is not approval of this substitution.
5. Check recording/purchase-sheet presentation before the final transaction. If needed, the owner performs the final purchase with computer control inactive. Confirm the capture is running before pressing the priced product button. If Apple's final Purchase control is missing, stop instead of consuming the tester without proof.

## Capture

1. Begin at the Mac desktop, launch Chronoframe and show About **2.0 (510)**.
2. Choose **Review Source** and **Review Destination**. Preview, approve the one-file verified transfer and show completed History and explicit Match verification. Original source bytes must match the baseline afterward.
3. Show all purchase entrances while the tester is still unowned. Do not commit any cleanup or Reorganize:
   - Settings → License → Unlock Chronoframe…; show the actual localized price and Restore, then Not Now.
   - Over-Allowance Source → Refused Destination: preview then request 501 copies, exceeding the fresh 500 allowance. Show the purchase/free-batch gate and dismiss without running the batch.
   - Deduplicate the **Duplicate Allowance Gate** disposable folder: review the 102 exact copies, select 101 removals and request cleanup, exceeding 100. Show the purchase gate, then dismiss without Trash. Verify every file remains.
   - Reorganize the one-file demo destination: request a changed layout and show its unlock entrance; dismiss without moves and restore the original layout choice.
4. For the final License safety demonstration, leave an Organize refusal pending in the main window, then open Settings → License → Unlock Chronoframe…. The voluntary License purchase must not resume that operation. Record destination inventory/receipt baseline before this step.
5. Confirm recording is running. Show the actual product, Apple's explicit sandbox/no-charge notice and visible Purchase control. Complete the sandbox purchase locally with credentials kept out of frame. If authentication is needed, pause the demonstration before revealing credentials and document any cut; do not describe edited footage as continuous.
6. Show Apple's success message, dismiss it, then License **Unlocked**. Return to the main window: no preview/transfer/Trash should start from the pending refusal. Do not approve a new operation. Quit/relaunch and show Unlocked again.
7. Exercise **Restore Purchases explicitly** on the actual signed app, separately recording its real outcome. An already-unlocked round trip does not prove a locked-to-unlocked restore, and automatic launch recovery is not this button test. Do not delete records to force a locked state.
8. Stop/save the capture and retain the original. Supply its local path; inspect the full footage for correct build, required flows, price, no-charge notice, successful purchase, unlock, persistence and privacy before attachment.

## Evidence and limitations

Record exact recording path, SHA-256, duration, resolution, candidate/source and which segments demonstrate each requirement. Inspect all footage, not only a contact sheet. A failed attempt or the old 508 diagnostic clip is not a successful-purchase demo. If screen capture prevents completion, preserve the failure and consider a separate physical-camera purchase clip with an explicit limitation in review notes; Apple's acceptance remains unresolved until confirmed or assessed explicitly at final owner Go. Do not mark the requested screen-recording gate passed solely from a phone clip.

Prepare the final build selection, direct app/IAP purchase navigation, real signed purchase screenshot and completed recording. Keep app and unlock product together, retain manual release, and obtain owner Go on the concrete candidate/evidence/limitations before submission.
