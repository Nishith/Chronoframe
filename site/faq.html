FAQ

The questions people ask first

If you're trusting an app with a lifetime of photos, you deserve straight answers.

Will Chronoframe change or delete my original photos?

Organize only reads your source folder. It never moves, renames, edits, or deletes those originals. Deduplicate is a separate workflow that moves only the files you approve in the scanned folder to macOS Trash.

Does it work with my Apple Photos library?

Yes. Open the Photos tab, authorize Photos access, pick the photos and videos you want, and choose Review & Import. Chronoframe copies the originals into your organized library through the same preview → confirm → verified-copy flow as everything else, and only ever reads your Photos library — it never changes, moves, favorites, or deletes anything there. Live Photos keep their photo and movie together, and iCloud-only originals are downloaded on demand. You can also point Chronoframe at loose files in folders or on drives.

Can I undo a run?

Yes. History can undo supported transfers, dedupe commits, and reorganize runs. Revert acts only when files still match the receipt, so it won't touch anything you added or changed afterward. For dedupe, the recorded files must still be in Trash with their recorded contents. Chronoframe cannot restore files after Trash is emptied.

What happens if a transfer is interrupted?

Each file is written safely to a temporary spot and only then put into place. Chronoframe records mutation state and reconciles interrupted work when you reopen it with the destination drive connected. History shows when a drive or manual action is still needed; your originals stay untouched.

Does Deduplicate work with videos?

Yes. Chronoframe finds byte-identical video copies by content. An off-by-default setting can also surface transcodes and re-exports that look alike; visual video matches always require your review.

Does Deduplicate permanently delete files?

No. Chronoframe moves only the files you approve to the macOS Trash. You review each duplicate group before committing, and hard delete is not part of the app.

How does Chronoframe figure out photo dates?

It checks photo metadata (EXIF capture time) first, then filename date patterns, then file creation and modification dates, and always defers to any date you set yourself in Review. Photos with an unknown date go to an Unknown_Date folder unless you provide one.

Do my photos ever leave my Mac?

Chronoframe processes photos on-device and never uploads your library. There is no Chronoframe account, analytics, or tracking. Cloud services may download originals or sync folders you choose; those transfers are controlled by their own services. See the privacy policy for details.

Does it work with iCloud or OneDrive folders?

Yes, but more slowly. Cloud drives aren't optimized for the file operations Chronoframe performs. Make sure originals are downloaded locally, and prefer local or external drives for the best performance.

What are the system requirements?

The trial release requires macOS 14.0 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. You'll need roughly as much free space as the size of your source folder, since Chronoframe copies files as-is.

What does Chronoframe cost?

Chronoframe is free to download. Try organizing 500 files and moving 100 approved duplicate files to Trash. A $14.99 USD one-time lifetime unlock removes those limits and includes Reorganize. There is no subscription or Chronoframe account. Regional prices vary; the app shows the local price before purchase.

How does the free trial work?

The 500-file organizing allowance and 100-file dedupe allowance are cumulative, per Apple Account per Mac. They have no time limit and do not renew each month. Counts refer to files, so paired resources such as a Live Photo’s still and movie can count separately.

Previews, plans, CSV exports, duplicate scans and review, Health, and History remain free and unlimited. Reorganizing an existing library into a different folder layout requires the unlock.

What happens when I use up the allowance?

Nothing is charged automatically. You can still preview, scan, review, and use History. To copy or move more files to Trash beyond the relevant allowance, choose the one-time unlock. Revert remains available without a purchase; successfully undoing trial work returns the corresponding allowance.

I already bought Chronoframe. Do I need to buy again?

No. Customers who bought the paid app keep full access. Use the same Apple Account; if the app doesn’t recognize your purchase, choose Restore Purchases in Settings before buying anything again. Get purchase help if it still looks wrong.

Do watched folders import automatically?

No. While Chronoframe is running, Organize → Sources watches registered folders and estimates new arrivals. You choose when to review and approve an import. A disconnected or incompletely scanned folder may need to be checked again.
