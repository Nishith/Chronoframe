Organize

From loose files to a library you can navigate

Setup, Run, Health, and History help you plan the transfer, check the result, and keep a record.

Step 1 · Setup

Point Chronoframe at the mess

Pick the folder to organize and a separate destination for the clean copies. Chronoframe reads the source and never mutates it. A contact sheet gives you a quick visual read of what's about to be organized before you commit to anything.

  • Default YYYY/MM/DD layout, or switch to year/month or event folders in Settings.
  • Source is read-only; the destination receives copies and receipts.
  • Prefer Apple Photos? Import selected originals into the same flow without changing your Photos library.

Step 2 · Run

Preview, then transfer

Preview Plan scans the source, resolves each date, and builds the transfer — without copying a thing. A timeline shows the shape of your library month by month, alongside counts for what's ready, already there, duplicated, or in need of attention. Correct or skip anything in Review, then start the transfer when the plan looks right.

  • Dates resolve from EXIF, then filename, then file dates — and always defer to any date you set.
  • Review uncertain dates before transferring; files without a resolved date can go into an Unknown_Date folder.

Step 2 · Run

Every frame lands, verified

With verification enabled (the default), each copy is re-hashed against the original. Files are written to a temporary location before being put into place. When the run finishes you get a summary of what was copied, skipped, and flagged — and a reminder that your originals were never touched. Reports and receipts are written to the destination for inspection or recovery.

Step 3 · Health

See what needs attention

The Health check scans for unknown dates, exact-duplicate hints, interrupted work, folder-structure drift, and anything that could make an undo unsafe. A single healthy score summarizes the state, and each card links you straight to the fix.

Step 4 · History

Every run is on the record

History keeps receipts for transfers, dedupe commits, and reorganize runs. Reuse a source you've organized before, change the destination layout, or revert a run — but only while the files still match the receipt, so an undo preserves files you added or changed afterward. Restoring a trashed file requires it to still be in Trash with matching contents; emptied Trash cannot be restored by Chronoframe.

Deduplicate

Reclaim space, one reviewed decision at a time

Find duplicates by content, review look-alikes side by side, and send only what you approve to the Trash.

Step 1 · Scan

Choose how thorough to be

Point Deduplicate at a folder and pick a detection preset. Balanced suits most libraries; Strict keeps things conservative; Loose casts a wider net. Exact photo and video copies are matched by content, so two files pair only when their bytes are identical — not because their names happen to match.

  • An off-by-default setting can surface visually similar videos (transcodes and re-exports) for review.
  • Recent scan folders remember what they recovered, so you can re-run in a click.

Step 2 · Review

You decide what stays

Each group is presented for a keep-or-delete decision, with the two frames side by side and details like size, dimensions, and sharpness to break ties. Automatic suggestions can help with high-confidence groups; weaker matches and visually similar videos need individual review. When the plan looks right, Move to Trash sends only your approved files to the macOS Trash — every mutation re-verified by content first, never a hard delete.

More ways to organize

Built around the places your photos live.

Apple Photos

Import originals directly.

Open Photos in the sidebar, grant access, select photos or videos, and choose Review & Import. Chronoframe exports the original, unedited resources to temporary staging, then uses the same preview and verified-copy workflow.

Live Photo stills and movies travel together. iCloud-only originals download through Apple, so allow a connection and extra temporary disk space. Nothing in your Apple Photos library is changed or deleted.

Watched sources

A standing list of what’s new.

Add a recurring source under Organize → Sources. While the app is running, Chronoframe watches for arrivals and estimates how many items are new.

You choose when to review and import. Watching a folder does not automatically copy files, change the destination, or skip your approval.

Photos and video

Find copies beyond filenames.

Exact matching compares file contents for both photos and videos. For possible re-exports and resizes, enable Find similar videos in Settings → Deduplicate → Video.

This optional setting is off by default and takes additional analysis. Similar-video groups always need your review; they never receive automatic removal selections.
