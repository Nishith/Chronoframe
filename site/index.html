Before you begin

A few things worth knowing.

Will it change my original photos?

Organize only reads the source folder and writes new copies to a separate destination. Deduplicate is a separate action: it moves only the files you approve in the folder you scan to macOS Trash.

Is this a subscription?

No. Download free, organize 500 files, and move 100 approved duplicate files to Trash. The allowance has no time limit. A $14.99 USD one-time unlock removes the limits and includes Reorganize. Regional prices may vary; the app shows your local price before purchase.

Does it work with Apple Photos?

Yes. Select photos and videos in Chronoframe’s Photos workspace, then review and import their unedited originals into dated folders. Your Apple Photos library stays unchanged. Deduplicate works on files in folders; it does not remove items from Apple Photos.

Does it handle videos and camera RAW files?

Yes. Chronoframe handles common photo and video formats, including JPEG, HEIC, camera RAW, MOV, and MP4. Exact video duplicates are matched by content. Similar-photo review and pair handling help you make more careful keep-or-remove decisions.

Do I need a cloud service or an internet connection?

Your photo processing happens on your Mac. No photos are uploaded to Chronoframe. Local processing works offline once purchase or trial access has been verified. The App Store may need a connection to verify access; importing iCloud-only originals also needs a download through Apple.

How much space will I need?

Allow space at the destination for the photos and videos you copy. Organize leaves the source in place, so it initially uses additional storage. Moving duplicates to Trash does not immediately free their space; macOS reclaims it when Trash is emptied, which also removes that recovery option.
