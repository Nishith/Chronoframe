Two jobs, one app

Bring order, then win back space

Organize turns chaos into a library you can navigate. Deduplicate reclaims storage from copies you didn't know you had. Both start with a preview, and both keep every decision in your hands.

Organize

Find any photo in seconds

Chronoframe copies your media into dated folders like 2024/06/15, working out each file's date from its metadata, its filename, and the file itself. Anything uncertain is flagged for review before a single file is copied. Your source folder is only ever read.

Deduplicate

Get your storage back

Find exact photo and video copies by content, not filename, then review similar shots, bursts, RAW+JPEG pairs, and Live Photos. You choose what to keep. Everything you approve moves to the macOS Trash — never a permanent delete.

Built to be trusted

Your photos are irreplaceable. Every step respects that.

No testimonials, no invented savings. Just the guarantees the app is built on, and a preview-first workflow you can see for yourself.

  • ✓Originals never changeChronoframe only reads your source. It never moves, renames, edits, or deletes an original.
  • ✓Nothing happens until you say goYou see exactly what will be copied, and what will move to Trash, before anything changes.
  • ✓Nothing gets overwrittenIf a name already exists at the destination, Chronoframe keeps both files instead of replacing one.
  • ✓Every copy is verifiedEach file is written safely and re-checked, so you know the copy matches the original byte for byte.
  • ✓Duplicates go to TrashFiles you approve for removal move to the macOS Trash. Hard delete is not part of the app.
  • ✓Interruptions recover safelyChronoframe records what it changed, reconciles on relaunch, and stops rather than guess when it can't verify state.

From the maker

Why I built Chronoframe

I had twenty years of photos spread across phones, laptops, and a drawer of drives, and I was terrified of touching them in case I lost something. The tools I found either wanted a monthly fee to hold my memories hostage, or deleted files in bulk and asked questions later.

So I built the app I wanted: one that copies, never moves your source files, shows you a plan before it does anything, and keeps receipts for safe undo. Chronoframe runs entirely on your Mac. Your media is never uploaded, and there's no account and no tracking — because it's yours, and it should stay that way.

Nishith Nand, maker of Chronoframe
